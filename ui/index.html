Disclosure Ledger

Private proof of prior discovery for security researchers, on Midnight

This page runs the compiled Compact circuits in your browser through @midnight-ntwrk/compact-runtime (WebAssembly), against an in-memory ledger. It is the same execution a DApp performs before proving. No wallet, no proof server, no network: zero-knowledge proofs are not generated here and nothing is sent anywhere.
